Salmon Kushiyaki

Level: Easy
Total Time: 6 hr 45 min
Active Time: 35 min
Yield: 4 servings

Description

This rendition of kushiyaki — the catch-all term for the popular Japanese technique of grilling skewered foods — is a play on a surf and turf, combining salmon with earthy shiitake mushrooms, for a dose of umami. Here the fish is first marinated in a mirin, sake and miso mixture; the same mixture is also brushed on the skewers during cooking for a burnished glaze. Finish the skewers with a generous shake of furikake or shichimi togarashi, two popular Japanese seasonings.

Ingredients

  • 1/4 cup mirin
  • 1/4 cup sake
  • 1/4 cup packed light brown sugar
  • 1/4 cup light miso
  • 1 1/2 pounds center-cut, skin-on salmon, cut into 1-inch cubes
  • 6 shiitake mushrooms, stemmed and quartered
  • Vegetable oil, for the grill grates
  • Kosher salt
  • Furikake or shichimi togarashi, for serving

Special Equipment

Four 10-inch bamboo paddle skewers

Instructions

  1. Soak four 10-inch bamboo paddle skewers in water for 30 minutes.
  2. Combine the mirin and sake in a small saucepan and bring to a boil over medium-high heat. Reduce to a simmer, add the brown sugar, and whisk until dissolved. Add the miso and whisk until dissolved. Let cool.
  3. Remove 1/4 cup of the marinade and set aside for brushing. Pour the remaining marinade over the salmon cubes, tossing to coat. Cover and refrigerate for at least 6 hours and preferably overnight.
  4. Prepare a grill for medium-high heat. Lightly coat the hot grates with vegetable oil.
  5. Remove the salmon from the marinade and blot dry with a paper towel. Thread each skewer with salmon and shiitake mushrooms, alternating between the two.
  6. Place the skewers on the grill and lightly sprinkle each with salt. Grill for about 1 minute on each side.
  7. Continue grilling and turning until the shiitake are tender and the salmon is browned and cooked through, about 10 minutes more.
  8. Brush the reserved miso marinade over each skewer. Grill for another 2 to 3 minutes until the glaze is slightly caramelized.
  9. Remove from the grill and sprinkle with furikake or shichimi togarashi.
