THE BAND

Meet the members of Evanescence and learn more about their roles in the band.

Amy Lee

Vocals, piano, keyboards

Amy Lee co-founded Evanescence in 1994 in Little Rock, Arkansas, and has been its lead singer and main songwriter ever since. She sings and plays piano, keyboards, and harp, blending classical and cinematic influences with heavy rock. She is the only member who has been with the band since the beginning.

Troy McLawhorn

Guitar

Plays guitar on stage and in the studio. Toured with the band from 2007 and rejoined officially in 2011 after a stint with Seether. He came from Dark New Day.

Tim McCord

Guitar (formerly bass)

Guitarist since 2022, and bass from 2006 to 2022. Before Evanescence he was in The Revolution Smile.

Will Hunt

Drums

Drummer for the band's live shows since 2007. Also from Dark New Day, and he has a co-writing credit on “Yeah Right” from The Bitter Truth.

Emma Anzai

Bass, backing vocals

Joined the band for their album Sanctuary. She previously played in Sick Puppies.
