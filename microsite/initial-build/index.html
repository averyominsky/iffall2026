Evanescence is an American rock band founded in 1994 by singer and keyboardist Amy Lee and guitarist Ben Moody in Little Rock, Arkansas. Their debut album, Fallen, came out in 2003 and included "Bring Me to Life" and "My Immortal." It sold more than four million copies in the US and earned the band two Grammy Awards.
